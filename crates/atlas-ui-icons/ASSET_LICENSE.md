# Atlas icon license

The SVG paths in `assets/` were created specifically for Atlas across its
development campaigns. They are released under CC0-1.0 and do not come from
the historical repository or a third-party icon set. The checksum registry is
the authoritative inventory; individual addition dates are intentionally not
claimed here.

Every asset uses a 24 × 24 `viewBox` and a monochrome silhouette so Slint can
apply semantic colors with `Image.colorize`.
